Founder & Creative Artisan

At Hunnar House, skill and heart come together. Founded with the conviction that true luxury lives in patient craftsmanship, our atelier in Bareilly is dedicated to making genuine gifts that bring real joy to homes.

We do not use assembly lines or artificial shortcuts. Soy wax is melted slowly, chocolates are tempered by sight and hand, and gift boxes are assembled with warm attention.

"When you gift something made by hand, you give someone a piece of calm, care, and human warmth."

Whether you need 5 signature candles for an intimate dinner or 200 tailored chocolate favor boxes for a family wedding, we speak to you directly and craft each one with patience.
