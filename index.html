Vastu Shastra

Balanced living and working spaces without structural demolition.

Practical Vastu Without Breaking Walls

Vastu is simply the practice of keeping spaces organized, well-lit, and balanced so they feel comfortable to live and work in. You do not need expensive renovations or demolition to correct your space.

  • Living Balance: Adjusting room usage, colors, and furniture orientation.
  • Work Focus: Setting up study desks and office chairs facing supportive directions.
  • Peaceful Rest: Ensuring bedrooms and sleep areas encourage healthy rest and recovery.

Mobile Numerology

Choosing numbers that support your work and daily interactions.

Why Your Phone Number Matters

We use our mobile phones continuously throughout the day for business calls, messages, client agreements, and family conversations. It is the number most closely tied to your everyday communication.

In Mobile Numerology, we review the total sum and sequence of digits in your contact number to check if they match your profession and birth numbers.

"A well-chosen contact number helps you maintain consistent, smoother professional and personal conversations."

What We Look At

  • Total Sum: Ensuring the final sum of your 10 digits matches your career goals.
  • Digit Repetitions: Avoiding repeating combinations that create frequent misunderstandings.
  • Profession Alignment: Recommending combinations suited specifically to business owners, salaried professionals, or creative workers.
  • Alternative SIM Suggestions: Helping you pick suitable new numbers from available telecom operator lists.
